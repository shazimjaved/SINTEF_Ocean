6. Working assumptions and next decisions
The next evidence requests are the cleaning and inspection history, including the last relevant event before the
observation period; confirmation of the clean-reference conditions and vessel configuration; torquemeter
calibration and operating records; and loading, weather and time-semantics metadata. These requests should
establish event completeness, clean-state provenance and comparable conditions without assigning unverified
clean labels. Task 2 explains the source-specific limitations. [A1, A2]
Stakeholders should agree the definition of “current condition,” the decision horizon, the responsible reviewer and
an acceptable uncertainty/alert policy. Economic evaluation additionally requires credible fuel-conversion
information, cleaning and downtime costs, and an intended operating profile. No particular cost or saving is
assumed.
The course's accepted scope should be confirmed with the professor/organizer while the Task 3 reply is pending.
The historical technical proposal is preserved for traceability in Task 2 v2, Appendix A; it has not been reassessed
against later work. This revision addresses Task 1 and Task 2 only. Data attribution and vessel confidentiality remain
part of the reporting requirements. [C3, C4]
