1. Dataset inventory
The dataset contains 41,079,968 sensor records in twelve 2024 monthly files and 1,112,405 records for January 2025,
giving 42,192,373 records across thirteen files. The registration identifies 37 QIDs: 36 occur in the 2024 data and 26 in
January 2025. The registered channel without observed values is a turbocharger RPM indicator. All torquemeter
channels, including shaft mechanical power, are absent in January 2025; it is not a labelled evaluation period for this
target. [R2, D1, D2]
The existing quality scan parsed all 42,192,373 observed sensor values as finite numeric values and identified no
malformed short records, numeric parsing failures or duplicate original (timestamp, QID) keys. Numeric completeness
within recorded rows does not establish coverage across calendar time. These are retained findings, not a newly
executed full-data audit. [R2]

The dictionary classifies the 36 observed channels as numeric after parsing and the unobserved registered channel as
not_observed. Heading and direction have circular semantics despite numeric storage. The 2024 source records are not
necessarily in chronological order; ordering must be handled explicitly. [R2, D1, D2]
Propulsion demand depends on speed through water, loading and trim, environmental resistance and operating state.
Speed over ground is also influenced by current. The SINTEF presentation identifies these influences and the
clean/current challenge calls for speed–power curves at different drafts. A defensible comparison therefore needs
condition matching and a trustworthy reference, alongside a reliable power measurement. [C1, C3]
