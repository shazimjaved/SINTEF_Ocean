3.1 Target-power anomalies
July 2024: 28,544 negative readings occur across fourteen dates. All target readings on 11, 12 and 15 July are negative,
and the longest near-contiguous negative sequence is 17,399 readings. At completed-minute resolution, 6,948
negative-power intervals coincide with positive CAMS engine RPM and speed through water above 3 kn. [R2]
November 2024: 656 negative readings occur across eight dates, with a minimum near −24,757 kW. The combined
negative-power/positive-engine-operation condition occurs in 93 completed-minute intervals. The minus sign is explicit
here and agrees with the supporting draft and validation record. [R2, M1, S1]
These patterns warrant separate target-quality investigation. They do not identify calibration errors, reversal, sign
convention or exact fault boundaries. The challenge warns of torque miscalibration in parts of the data; corresponding
operation/calibration records are still needed. Negative values have not been silently clipped or replaced in this revision.
June's absence of negative readings is not proof of clipping. December has 166 negative readings; the historical
evaluation-policy caveat is preserved in Appendix A. [C3, R2]
Trustworthy target measurements are essential for comparison with a clean reference: a sensor shift can appear as a
power difference. Likewise, an engine signal that closely follows power may support reconstruction while reflecting
current degradation. Neither an apparently plausible estimate nor a strong association establishes clean-condition
demand. Figures 2–4 retain the existing coverage evidence. [R2, A1]
