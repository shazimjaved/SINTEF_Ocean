Appendix A. Preserved technical proposal from 25 October 2026
This appendix relocates technical specifications from delivered Task 1 §§1–5 and Task 2 §5. It preserves their historical
status as proposals; it does not reassess them against later work, report experiments or establish a clean-condition
model. Current Task 3 decisions remain pending and outside this revision. [R1, R2]
A.1 Target and reconstruction formulation
The original task is supervised regression of Vessel Propeller Shaft Mechanical Power (kW), registered QID:
2::0::11::0_1::1::0::3::0_14::0::1::0_8
Each example describes a completed one-minute interval. Onboard measurements are summarized at its end; the target
is recorded power for that interval. This is not a prediction of the following minute, an instantaneous estimate at the
beginning of the minute, or a demonstrated route-optimization system. January 2025 has no target for labelled
evaluation. Provider data use the past-timestamp rule in §2, with availability unresolved. [R1, R2]
The original exclusions cover torque, mechanical energy, thrust, shaft revolutions, shaft RPM and torquemeter-sourced
fuel mass flow; four zero-draft channels; and initially water depth because of absent October overlap. The 24 candidates
include engine RPM, turbocharger RPM, scavenging pressure, fuel load, vessel speeds/motion, relative wind and 14
provider channels. These source labels/roles do not prove operational independence or a valid clean-condition feature
set. [R1, M1]

A.3 Historical technical success measures
The original proposal specifies MAE and RMSE in kW, supplementary R², observation counts,
time-block/operating-condition reporting and simple baselines: a training-mean estimate, a speed-based heuristic fitted
only on training data, and persistence only when a preceding trusted power observation would actually be available. It
seeks meaningful improvement on untouched chronological data. Operational tolerance requires stakeholder
requirements; model accuracy alone cannot establish maintenance value. No metric values or achieved improvements
are asserted. [R1]
